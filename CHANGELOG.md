# Changelog

## v1.0.0

The first release of Keyscape, live at [keyboardcheat.codes](https://keyboardcheat.codes).

### Keyboard
- A 3D keyboard you can type on with your own keyboard or by tapping the keys. Drag around it to tilt it.
- Five sizes: 60%, 65%, 75%, TKL and full-size with a numpad.
- Four keycap shapes: Cherry, SA, DSA and low profile.
- Seven colorways: Tidepool, Ember, Sakura, Matcha, Office beige, Mono and Signal.
- A green light on every key you've pressed, for checking that a keyboard works.

### Sound board
- Six switch sounds: Thock, Clack, Clicky, Creamy, Marbly and Typewriter.
- Case material, volume and pitch controls, plus a typing demo.

### Shortcut tester
- 20 common shortcuts, adapted for Mac or Windows, that tick off as you find them.
- A live log of what your keyboard sends for each key.

### Games
- Chase the light: hit the glowing key, 30 seconds.
- Type sprint: a 30-second typing speed test.

### Site
- Light, Dark, High contrast and Minimal themes.
- Your settings and best scores are saved in your browser.
- Works on desktop, tablet and phone.
